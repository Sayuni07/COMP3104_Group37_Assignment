# George Brown Polytechnic

## George Brown Polytechnic History
George Brown Polytechnic takes its name from George Brown, a Scottish-born Canadian politician
who lived from 1818 to 1880. Brown was a firm advocate of public education and the founder of 
The Globe newspaper. In 1967, Ontario Education Minister Bill Davis proposed the name for the 
new college of applied arts and technology, arguing that Toronto should honour a reformer, 
journalist, and statesman who had brought distinction to the city. The college opened in 1968 
with just over 2,000 students.
