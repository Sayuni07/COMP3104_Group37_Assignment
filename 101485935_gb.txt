George Brown College is a public post-secondary institution in Toronto, Ontario, Canada. The college offers programs in technology, business, health sciences, design, and other fields. It emphasizes practical learning, career preparation, and industry connections. Students can develop technical knowledge and professional skills through coursework, projects, and collaborative activities.

The college provides students with opportunities to learn through practical assignments, group projects, and career-focused activities. These experiences help students strengthen communication, problem-solving, teamwork, and technical skills that are useful in the workplace.

Career preparation is an important part of college education. Students can build professional portfolios, practise presenting their work, and develop skills that employers value. Group assignments also help students learn how to coordinate tasks and meet deadlines.
