George Brown College - Overview
================================

About the College
-----------------
George Brown College is a public college of applied arts and technology located in
downtown Toronto, Ontario. It was founded in 1967 and is named after George Brown,
a Toronto newspaper publisher (founder of The Globe) and one of the Fathers of
Confederation.

Campuses
--------
The college operates three main campuses in the heart of the city:
- St. James Campus - business, arts and design, hospitality and culinary arts
- Casa Loma Campus - technology, construction and engineering technologies
- Waterfront Campus - health sciences, built on Toronto's waterfront

Programs and Learning Style
---------------------------
George Brown offers certificates, diplomas, advanced diplomas, degrees and
graduate certificates. Its focus is career-ready, hands-on learning: many programs
include labs, real client projects, co-op placements and field work so students
graduate with practical experience employers are looking for.

School of Computer Technology
-----------------------------
I am a student in the Computer Programming and Analysis (T177) advanced diploma.
The program covers software development, web and mobile apps, databases, networking
and DevOps practices, and finishes with a capstone project built by student teams.

Why It Matters to Me
--------------------
Being in downtown Toronto puts students close to one of Canada's largest tech
markets. Team projects like this assignment mirror how real software teams work,
which makes the transition from school to industry much smoother.
