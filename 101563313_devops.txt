# COMP3104 - DevOps 

COMP3104 DevOps is a hands-on course designed to show you what it’s actually like to work as 
a DevOps engineer.We will be working with essential tools like Git, GitHub, Docker, Jenkins, 
and SonarQube while learning how modern tech teams build, test, and ship reliable software quickly.


Throughout the course, we’ll dive into microservices vs. monoliths, set up automated CI/CD 
pipelines, practice safe branching strategies, and explore cloud infrastructure. Through a mix of 
lectures, practical labs, and assignments, you’ll build real-world skills and wrap up the term with
a clear picture of DevOps career paths and market demand.
