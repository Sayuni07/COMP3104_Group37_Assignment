The Importance of DevOps in the Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)
=====================================================================

What is the SDLC?
-----------------
The software development lifecycle is the process teams follow to build software:
planning, requirements, design, development, testing, deployment and maintenance.
In traditional (waterfall) models these phases happen one after another and are
often owned by separate teams, which slows feedback and causes hand-off problems.

Where DevOps Fits
-----------------
DevOps turns the SDLC into a continuous loop - plan, code, build, test, release,
deploy, operate, monitor - and repeats it in small, frequent cycles. Developers and
operations share ownership of the product from the first line of code to production.
